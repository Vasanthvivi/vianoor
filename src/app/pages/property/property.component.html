La Montanha is a gated community of 16 private villas in Goa situated in the laid-back coastal town of Ashwem. A short walk from the wide, flat sands and calm waters of the Arabian Sea, the estate is set back from the village’s main roads and surrounded by tropical vegetation. Despite its quiet location, it remains close to local favourites like Saz on the Beach, Verandah, and Palm Grove, offering easy access to local food, music, and everyday essentials.

These villas near the beach are influenced by Indo-Portuguese architecture, with sloping roofs, arched doorways, and wood-framed windows that reflect the visual heritage of the region. Details like Juliette balconies with intricate metalwork and shaded verandahs add both texture and utility. Each home spans two floors and is designed around openness—multiple windows, skylights, and glass doors invite in light and cross-breezes, creating an airy, grounded atmosphere that shifts with the time of day.

Each residence at La Montanha features a private pool and garden, bordered by dense flora that creates a sense of seclusion within nature. Mornings are perfect for coffee on the upstairs terrace, while evenings lend themselves to quiet swims or relaxed gatherings under the verandah, as the sea breeze drifts in.

The community is designed to feel peaceful and self-contained while still being part of North Goa’s vibrant coastal atmosphere. Whether visiting for a short stay or settling in for the long term, La Montanha offers a place to unwind and enjoy a slower pace without losing touch with the energy of the surrounding area.

A dedicated property management team handles day-to-day practicalities, including maintenance, furnishing, and rental support, making ownership effortless. For those exploring luxury villas for sale in North Goa, La Montanha combines privacy, comfort, and an authentic connection to the bohemian spirit of Ashwem.
